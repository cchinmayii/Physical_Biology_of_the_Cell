\section{Notation summary}
\label{app:notation}

Variables specific to a single biological model are defined where that model is introduced.

$\Delta t$, $\Delta x$ & Finite increments in time or position. \\

$\mathrm{d}/\mathrm{d}t$ & Ordinary derivative with respect to time, used when the quantity depends on a single independent variable. \\

$\partial/\partial t$, $\partial/\partial x$ & Partial derivatives, used when a quantity depends on several variables. \\

$\mathcal{O}(\epsilon^n)$ & Terms of order $\epsilon^n$ or smaller that are omitted from an approximation. \\

$\approx$, $\sim$, $\propto$ & Approximately equal to, scales as, and proportional to, respectively. \\

$\langle X\rangle$ & Mean or expectation value of the quantity $X$. \\

$\operatorname{Var}(X) = \langle X^2\rangle-\langle X\rangle^2$ & Variance of $X$. \\

% $ & Variance of $X$, written explicitly as a named operation. \\

$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$ & Covariance of $X$ and $Y$. \\

$\mu$, $\sigma$, $\sigma^2$ & Mean, standard deviation, and variance of a probability distribution. \\

$p_i$ & Probability assigned to the discrete state $i$. \\

$p(x)$ & Probability density for a continuous variable $x$. \\

$\sum_i p_i=1$, $\int p(x)\,\dd x=1$ & Normalization conditions for discrete and continuous probability distributions. \\

$[X]$ & Concentration of the molecular species $X$. \\

$\mathrm{ss}$ & Subscript denoting a steady-state value. \\

$\mathrm{tot}$ & Subscript denoting the total amount or concentration of a conserved quantity. \\

$\mathrm{on}$, $\mathrm{off}$ & Subscripts denoting association and dissociation processes or the corresponding states and times. \\

% $\lambda$ & A rate, the mean of a Poisson distribution, or an eigenvalue, depending on context. \\

$\mathbf{x}$, $\mathbf{p}$ & Vectors; bold lowercase symbols denote collections of components. \\

$A$, $C$, $X$ & Matrices or linear operators, as specified in the surrounding text. \\

% $A^{\mathsf T}$, $A^{-1}$ & Transpose and inverse of the matrix $A$. \\

$\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}$ & Estimated value of a parameter vector. \\

$\delta\mathbf{x}$ & Deviation of a vector from its mean, $\delta\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{x}-\langle\mathbf{x}\rangle$. \\

$\delta(x-x_0)$ & Dirac delta distribution concentrated at $x=x_0$. \\

$D$ & Diffusion constant. \\

$J$ & Flux: the amount of material crossing a unit area per unit time. \\

$k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ & Boltzmann constant. \\

$T$ & Absolute temperature. \\

$\beta=1/(k_BT)$ & Inverse thermal energy. \\
% , unless introduced as a Lagrange multiplier. \\

$E$, $S$, $F$ & Energy, entropy, and free energy. \\

$Z$ & Partition function: the sum of the statistical weights of all allowed states. \\

\end{longtable}
